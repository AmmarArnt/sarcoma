⟳ Uncontrolled Proliferation
No apoptosis. No differentiation. Infinite loop running.
Key constraint: CIC-DUX4 itself cannot be neutralized by dietary means — it is a structural genomic event. Food-based compounds work on the amplification context around it: reducing super-enhancer gain, rebalancing chromatin, and supporting apoptotic thresholds — not fixing the fusion. Think of it as reducing the loop's execution speed, not terminating the loop.
Point A — Upstream RAS/ERK Dampening
Reduce the signal feeding the loop
Can't fix CIC-DUX4 structurally, but reducing upstream ERK amplitude lowers co-activator and BRD4 recruitment to super-enhancers. Less signal input = less loop amplification. Slows execution speed.
Quercetin Resveratrol Omega-3 EPA/DHA Lycopene Berberine
Quercetin (onions, capers): inhibits RAS activation & RTK signaling. Omega-3: disrupts membrane lipid raft clustering needed for RAS assembly. Berberine: AMPK activator, broadly suppresses MAPK amplitude. Lycopene (tomatoes): downregulates RAS/ERK pathway activity in multiple models.
Point B — BRD4 / Super-Enhancer Disruption
Throttle the transcription factory
CIC-DUX4 drives ETS targets by recruiting BRD4 to super-enhancers. Compounds with BET inhibitor-like activity displace BRD4 from acetylated histones, collapsing the 10–100× amplification layer sitting on top of the fusion's output.
EGCG (green tea) Curcumin Apigenin Kaempferol
EGCG: documented BRD4 inhibitory activity — reduces super-enhancer-driven MYC. Curcumin: disrupts BRD4 binding to acetylated chromatin, affects H3K27ac deposition. Apigenin (parsley, chamomile): shown to reduce ETS factor expression in multiple cancer models.
Point C — Chromatin Rebalancing / HDAC Axis
Reopen silenced tumor suppressor code
Sarcoma cells silence differentiation and apoptosis genes via HDAC-mediated chromatin compaction. Natural HDAC inhibitors partially reopen this chromatin — think of it as partially restoring read-access to locked files. Reactivates some apoptosis & differentiation pathways that oppose the loop.
Sulforaphane DIM (cruciferous) Butyrate (fiber) Allicin (garlic)
Sulforaphane (broccoli sprouts): HDAC inhibitor & NRF2 activator — most studied dietary epigenetic modulator. Butyrate: produced by gut fermentation of fiber — validated HDAC inhibitor, promotes apoptosis. DIM: modulates H3 acetylation broadly, supports differentiation signaling.
Point D — ETS Targets & Cell Cycle Downstream
Pressure the output, not the source
Even if the loop structurally can't be stopped, slowing CDK4/CCND1 velocity and reactivating apoptosis thresholds downstream creates counterpressure. Reduces how fast the loop executes per unit time. Not a fix — a friction layer.
Genistein (soy) Fisetin (strawberries) Luteolin (celery) Selenium Zinc
Genistein: inhibits CDK activity, promotes G2/M cell cycle arrest. Fisetin (strawberries, apples): ETS factor inhibition, CDK4 suppression documented. Selenium: required for selenoproteins modulating redox balance & apoptosis thresholds — deficiency directly reduces apoptotic efficiency in cancer cells.
