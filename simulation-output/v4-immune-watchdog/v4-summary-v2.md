**Summary:** Restores immune *visibility* and *clearance* of fusion-positive cells across four arms — checkpoint/T-cell, NK missing-self, microbiome-immune, neoantigen-vaccine — now **natively integrating the ADR-0006 expansion** (danger-signaling/immunogenic cell death, the Nectin–TIGIT–DNAM-1 axis, NKG2A/HLA-E, NK exhaustion/stress-ligand evasion) and the **inflammation-state lens**. It consumes V3's MHC-I bridge (**rerouted off the withdrawn tazemetostat** to valemetostat/MAK683/entinostat) and the mRNA-v2 brief. Tracks are separated; three scoring axes applied. Anchored to a fusion-unconfirmed patient with prior WLI and imminent high-dose ifosfamide.

**Confidence: Low–Moderate (whole output).** Checkpoint/NK/ICD/Nectin biology is well-established at the class level, but **direct CIC-DUX4 immune data are essentially absent (D = − almost everywhere)**; sarcoma checkpoint-monotherapy ORR is modest (SARC028); and the most advanced innate-brake program (anti-TIGIT *receptor* blockade) **failed its phase-3 readouts**. The **NK missing-self arm is the mechanistically strongest non-obvious lever** because it exploits — rather than fights — the MHC-I-low state.

**Patient case (clean-slate; no stored personal memory).** Fusion-UNCONFIRMED atypical ~5% subgroup; biceps femoris primary; 12 lung mets at dx → EURO EWING (VDC/IE) ×14 → surgery (>95% necrosis) → leg + whole-lung irradiation → NED 5/25–5/26 → **single-lung oligometastatic relapse 5/26** → **high-dose ifosfamide imminent** (profound lymphodepletion → plan immune steps for the reconstitution window).

## THE INFLAMMATION-STATE LENS (standing V4 discipline — ADR-0006)

| State | Biology | Marker | What helps | A blanket "reduce inflammation" does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **(1) Tumor-promoting** | NF-κB/STAT3, IL-6, TNF, COX-2/PGE₂, MDSC/M2 | ↑CRP/NLR/IL-6 (chronic) | **suppress** it | **beneficial** |
| **(2) Anti-tumor activation** | IFN-γ, CXCL9-11, cytotoxic CD8/NK, productive ICD | "hot"/immunoscore-high (acute) | **amplify** it (ICD, checkpoint, IL-15) | **harmful** — blunts what you want |
| **(3) Treatment toxicity** | irAE (pneumonitis/colitis), CRS, mucositis | iatrogenic | **manage** (steroids/tocilizumab) | necessary for safety but a confound — looks like (2) on CRP, means the opposite |

**Operational consequence:** "lower inflammation" can move (1) *and* (2) the wrong way at once — this is exactly why the framework encodes the ATBC/CARET/SELECT and NAC-metastasis cautions (V2). For this patient, post-WLI pneumonitis risk = State (3); the relapse TME = State (1); the goal of any V4 step is State (2). **Lowering inflammation ≠ improving anti-tumor immunity.**

## CASE-SPECIFIC SEQUENCING (oncologist discussion only)
1. **Ifosfamide → profound lymphodepletion.** Plan immune steps for the **reconstitution window (wks ~4–8)**, not during cytotoxic therapy.
2. **NK-vs-MHC-I tension (do not paper over):** NK killing wants MHC-I-**low**; T-cell killing wants MHC-I-**high**; epigenetic restoration helps T-cells but blunts NK *and may co-induce HLA-E* (a second brake). **Suggested order: NK-first (exploit MHC-I-low: N-803 / anti-PVR / adoptive NK) → EZH2-pathway/HDACi MHC-I restoration (paired with anti-NKG2A) → T-cell checkpoint.**
3. **Relapse sits in a post-WLI, plausibly STING-primed lung niche** — a rationale for checkpoint at that site (Forward Hyp 2).
4. **Tissue step:** if archived/relapse tissue available, WES+RNA-seq for somatic (non-junction) neoantigen feasibility + anti-PEG titer first.

## FORWARD HYPOTHESES

**[Forward Hypothesis 1] NK-first ligand-side de-repression (anti-PVR), *not* TIGIT-receptor blockade, in the MHC-I-low window.** Because CIC cells are plausibly MHC-I-low and TIGIT-receptor programs failed, block tumor **PVR/CD155** (NTX1088-class) *before* any MHC-I restoration — simultaneously lifting TIGIT/CD96/PVRIG and restoring DNAM-1, while the MHC-I-low state still favors NK. *Falsifier:* in a CIC-DUX4 PDX/NK-humanized model, anti-PVR ± IL-15 produces **no** increase in NK infiltration/cytotoxicity or tumor control vs control, or CIC cells prove MHC-I-normal (removing the missing-self premise). *Why untested:* no CIC-DUX4 NK model; anti-PVR itself Phase 1.

**[Forward Hypothesis 2] ICD-adjuvanted anthracycline timing — schedule the *existing* doxorubicin-emitted danger window, then capture it.** If doxorubicin already induces ICD, the lever is *timing* an immune-capture step (DC-licensing/checkpoint/NK) into the post-anthracycline CALR/HMGB1/ATP window — no new drug. *Falsifier:* CIC-DUX4 cells show no CALR exposure / HMGB1 / ATP release under anthracycline (ICD-incompetent), or in-phase vs out-of-phase capture shows no difference in antigen-specific CD8 priming. *Why untested:* ICD scheduling unstudied in CIC sarcoma; steroid/antioxidant confound rarely controlled.

**[Forward Hypothesis 3] HLA-E/NKG2A is the predictable escape valve of epigenetic MHC-I restoration.** EZH2i/HDACi restore *classical* MHC-I but may **co-induce HLA-E**, re-suppressing both the NK arm relied on earlier and the new CD8 arm — so pair MHC-I restoration with **anti-NKG2A (monalizumab)**, not anti-PD-1 alone. *Falsifier:* CIC-DUX4 lines ± EZH2i/HDACi show classical MHC-I↑ **without** HLA-E co-induction, or adding anti-NKG2A does not improve NK/CTL killing. *Why untested:* HLA-E response to epigenetic therapy uncharacterized in this disease.

**[Forward Hypothesis 4] Post-ifosfamide reconstitution + radiation-primed lung as a convergent immunotherapy window.** Lymphodepletion (homeostatic IL-7/15 expansion) + prior-WLI STING-primed niche + single residual lesion → initiate MHC-I priming (wks 4–6) then checkpoint (wks 6–8). *Falsifier:* TCR-repertoire/neoantigen-reactive frequency does not rise in the reconstitution window, or prior-WLI vs no-WLI strata show no ORR difference. *Why untested:* window-of-opportunity designs rare in this rare histotype.
