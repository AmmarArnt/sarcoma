# CIC-Rearranged Sarcoma Research Simulation

An engineer-led, AI-assisted research project that explores CIC-rearranged sarcoma (CIC-DUX4 fusion) through a systems and software-engineering lens.

## What this repository is
- A structured research simulation, not a treatment protocol.
- A framework for generating and ranking mechanistic intervention hypotheses with evidence tiers.
- A way to organize complex oncology reasoning using a multi-agent architecture.

## Core idea
The project models the disease program as a multi-layer systems problem and investigates four intervention vectors in parallel:
1. Rate limiting oncogenic output
2. Protecting genomic stability in neighboring at-risk cells
3. Restoring suppressor signaling and differentiation in fusion-positive cells
4. Improving immune visibility and clearance

Outputs are synthesized into a ranked hypothesis set intended for further scientific review.

## Why this exists
The goal is to make research thinking explicit, testable, and iteratively improvable by combining:
- engineering-style decomposition,
- AI-driven parallel analysis,
- literature-grounded evidence grading.

## Collaboration direction
This work is intentionally positioned as an evolving thesis. As it matures, the intent is to collaborate with medical researchers, clinicians, and relevant health authorities to validate assumptions, refine methods, and align future directions with real-world clinical and regulatory standards.
